# History of AI timeline (Reed Library, SUNY Fredonia)

An interactive, cited history of artificial intelligence through technology, ideas and fiction, pop culture and art, society and policy, education, and SUNY's own history. Every card links to sources, and cards cite items from Reed Library's collection. Curated by Christina Hilburger, Reed Library.

This project is separate from any other project in this account.

## What is here

- `dist/embed.html`: the snippet to paste into a LibGuides Media/Widget item. It loads the three files below.
- `dist/ail-timeline.css`, `dist/ail-timeline.js`: styles and code.
- `dist/timeline-data.json`: the milestones (built from `source/`).
- `source/`: the milestone data and the build script.

The files in `dist/` are served by GitHub Pages at `https://chilburger.github.io/reed-ai-timeline/dist/`.

## Adding milestones

New milestones can be added as rows in the Google Sheet "AI Timeline additions (Reed Library)"; the timeline shows them as "Recently added" once the sheet is shared as "Anyone with the link can view". Researched milestones are added to `source/TIMELINE-CONTENT.json` and rebuilt.

## Accessibility

Keyboard and screen reader support, reduced motion, captions and transcript notes for media, and a plain list of every milestone below the timeline (also used for print).

## Services for other libraries

The timeline is free to reuse under the licenses above. Reed Library plans to offer services at cost-recovery rates, tiered by institution (SUNY campuses lowest, then other public institutions, then private institutions): hosting on Reed's network, a campus's own collection and catalog links, new subject timelines, and training and support. Contributions help sustain the work and the librarians who curate it. Details to come.
